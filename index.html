Henka Crop

Square listing photos from your card shots

Choose the photos you took. Each card is found, straightened and centered on a 2000 × 2000 photo, ready for eBay or the website. It all happens on this phone: nothing is uploaded.

  • Shoot each card on a plain, dark surface, one card per photo.
  • Leave a little space around the card; it's fine if the phone is a bit tilted.
  • Avoid glare: turn the card or the light until the shine is gone.
